## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I build software end-to-end — from API and backend architecture to the interface on top of it. Most of my work lives where software meets the physical world: computer vision that reads industrial gauges, AR applications that guide maintenance on real machines, and the web platforms around them. I also ship games and 3D experiences in Unity and Unreal Engine — some published on Google Play and itch.io.

- 🔭 **Now:** AR & computer-vision developer at **TDS**, building industrial maintenance tooling
- 🎓 **Education:** Bachelor's in Big Data & Data Analysis — ISIMS, Sfax (2022–2026)
- 📍 **Based in** Sfax, Tunisia
- 🎮 **Shipped:** games and AR apps on Google Play & itch.io (The356Company)
